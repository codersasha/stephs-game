How to Play

🐱

Welcome, Young Warrior!

You are a kit in your clan. Your goal is to survive, grow strong, and become the leader!

❤️ 🍖 💧

Stay Alive!

Watch your bars at the top:

  • ❤️ Health - Don't let this reach zero!
  • 🍖 Hunger - Hunt to fill this up
  • 💧 Thirst - Drink water to stay hydrated
🎮

Use the Buttons!

Tap the buttons at the bottom to do things:

  • 🎯 Hunt - Catch prey to eat
  • 💧 Drink - Get water
  • 👁️ Patrol - Guard your territory
  • 😴 Rest - Heal and grow older
⭐

Rank Up!

As you grow, your rank changes:

  • Kit → Apprentice (at 6 moons)
  • Apprentice → Warrior (with experience)
  • Warrior → Deputy → Leader!
